## Core search rule

FB-05 should not assume closure requires one perfect RH-sized lemma. The preferred target is a **same-state incompatibility**:

```text
RH false
  -> exists one exact retained canonical state x
  -> A(x)
  -> B(x)

prove independently:
  A(x) -> C(x)
  B(x) -> not C(x)

therefore x cannot exist.
```

Permanent composition rule:

```text
same state
same aperture
same parity
same normalization
same production object
```

A contradiction between statements about different witnesses is not a contradiction until the project has a theorem identifying the objects strongly enough to compose them.

Informal mnemonic: an RH-false state may be forced to **fly through the air and swim underwater at the same time**. The goal is to theoremize two modest requirements whose intersection is empty.

## Why zero-density percentages are not enough

The 67.25% theorem is compatible with a sparse exceptional off-line set. Counting alone cannot exclude one off-line quartet.

The useful target must instead be same-window / same-test / same-state, e.g.

```text
positive contribution forced by sufficiently many nearby simple critical zeros
    >
maximum negative contribution available to the exceptional/off-line sector
```

for the special canonical witness forced by first-bad geometry. Such a restricted statement can in principle be much narrower than full Weil positivity.

## Falsification firewall

- **Sparse-exception test:** if the argument fails when off-line zeros have density zero, percentage counting is insufficient.
- **Independence test:** do not double-count two proofs of the same second-moment phenomenon.
- **Same-object test:** both properties must apply to the exact same state/aperture/parity/normalization/production object.
- **Normalization test:** external or research coordinate systems require exact bridges before composition.
- **Exceptional-budget test:** if a tiny off-line set can contribute arbitrarily large negative mass, density information cannot dominate it.
- **Mustache test:** reject any allegedly modest lemma that unfolds to an RH-equivalent target such as `ArithmeticSideSubexponential` or full Weil positivity.
- **Moving-state test:** do not silently drop derivative terms from a moving optimizer; the envelope cancellation must be theoremized at the exact interface.
- **Global-monotonicity test:** PR #182 licenses local contact calculus, not DR-022 global fixed-sign Schur monotonicity.
- **Circularity test:** neither side may assume successor positivity, absence of the negative root, or RH-equivalent content.
