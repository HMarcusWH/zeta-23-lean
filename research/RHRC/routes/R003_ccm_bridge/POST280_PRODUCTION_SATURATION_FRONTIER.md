**Claim cap:** `EXPERIMENTAL_SIGNAL_ONLY`  
**Terminal claim:** `RH_OPEN`

algebra to one exact arithmetic remainder comparison. The theorem-backed
positive quantity is

\[
Q(v)=\Re(\overline{S(v)}M_4(v)),
\]

with the same quantity represented spectrally by the opposite-parity energy
after subtracting the selected eigenvalue term.

The unresolved production equality is measured through

\[
\Delta_{\rm sat}
=
\mathcal A_L[R]

\frac{(2\pi)^2}{L^2}Q(v).
\]

This protocol does **not** assert that any finite sampled state is a generated
first contact. Values away from a proved zero/stationary contact are labelled
near-contact proxies.
