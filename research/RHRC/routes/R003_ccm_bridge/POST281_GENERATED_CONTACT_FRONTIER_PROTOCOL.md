# Post-#281 generated production contact frontier

Status: research protocol. Claim cap: **EXPERIMENTAL_SIGNAL_ONLY**. RH remains OPEN.

This protocol consumes merged-green PR #281 and the generated-contact theorem
surface built in PR #282. It deliberately separates broad floating discovery
from bounded Arb certification.

## Frozen discovery

Every physical integer cutoff cell Q=1..64 is scanned, not only prime-power
thresholds. K=2..6, both parity sectors and interior fractions j/16 are used.
Q=1 starts at L=1/512. Canonical, planted-on-line and planted-off-line models
share the same front-end evaluator. The repaired #281 99-row panel is replayed
separately and remains immutable historical evidence.

Candidate selection is deterministic. Sign brackets have first priority,
followed by small absolute global bottom, small parity separation and small
within-sector gap. Selection by rho proximity to one or by a preferred
saturation-gap sign is forbidden.

## Certification

Selected candidates are replayed at 192/384/768-bit Arb precision using the
existing fixed-Q analytic M,M',M'' backends. Interval eigenvalues are
authoritative for bounded numerical signs. Approximate ground vectors are
accepted only with a residual/separation error budget; otherwise derivative
quantities are unresolved.

A local bracket is not the first global boundary. A failed search is
CONTACT_NOT_LOCATED, not a no-contact theorem. A numerical interval containing
zero is not exact stationarity.

The later remainder, response, curvature, Delta and rho fields inherit
independent resolution states. A resolved Q does not resolve a cancellation
sensitive Delta.

Planted controls are local falsifiers, not alternate zeta functions or Euler
products. Generic matrix controls do not inherit canonical explicit-formula
authority.

## Interventions and generic controls

For each frozen selected canonical location the discovery artifact also evaluates the inherited #281 floating source/remainder/Delta proxy. The current-Q experiment is split into two non-interchangeable records:

- frozen-state ablation: hold L,K and the original selected vector fixed while removing only the current-Q prime atom;
- reoptimized ablation: remove that atom and recompute the parity grounds.

Exact zero-von-Mangoldt controls must be no-ops. These are floating research diagnostics, not theorem authority.

Generic transverse, stationary-cubic, positive-quartic-touch, parity-tie, odd-reflected and smallest-complement controls are emitted under GENERIC_SYNTHETIC provenance. They do not inherit the production explicit formula.

The Arb layer currently certifies spectral/first-derivative sign information and local sign brackets. Q, the physical remainder, stationary response, optimized curvature, Delta and rho are not promoted to rigorous generated-contact enclosures until their exact formal bridge and independent interval implementations exist.
