C3 has an explicit analytic counterexample at epsilon four. Choose an even
smooth probability bump beta supported in `[-1/100,1/100]` and
`phi=(4/5)beta+(1/10)beta(.-7/4)+(1/10)beta(.+7/4)`.
Its autocorrelation is supported inside `[-4,4]`. Write `f=Delta*psi` and
`w=sinc^2*|Fourier(phi)|^2`. Then `f(3)>0` and
`Fourier(phi)(5/2)=Fourier(beta)(5/2)*(4/5-sqrt(2)/10)>0`.
For `nu=delta_(5/2)+c delta_3`, choose
`c=(w(5/2)-f(5/2))/f(3)`. The tested defect is exactly zero, the gap is one,
both stated local variation windows have zero mass, and off-lattice negative
mass is zero; nevertheless the positive tested mass is nonzero. The original
first inequality therefore fails. This is DERIVED analytic mathematics, not
a Lean-formalized counterexample.

Proposed supporting-contract repairs are explicit: restrict epsilon to at most
one, or use the smoothing window `[-1-epsilon,1+epsilon]`. The second displayed
bound already requires epsilon at most `1/(2*pi*R)` with R at least one and
therefore avoids this defect. The source amendment is retained unchanged.

C4 must quantify over a fixed measure and full distributional self-duality
