## 5. Mathematical implications and adversarial checks

**DERIVED from the recorded formulas:** the zero condition is concentrated in the sharp boundary coefficient and its order of vanishing. The generic forced family exists without a zero premise. Zero multiplicity kills precisely the lower boundary jets. This compresses the dependency graph but supplies no independent contradiction.

**LEAD:** after proving H2a/H2b, use negative-mass budgets as a quantitative obstruction on a fixed actual basis, then ask whether source-derived coefficient bounds or separated-node estimates yield subexponential distortion. The strongest unsupported jump would be from fixed-dimensional compactness to a growing basis. The determinant contains powers of node differences; generic node collisions immediately warn that conditioning can degenerate. Generic near-collision matrices are a normalization/conditioning test, not fabricated actual zeta zeros.

**LEAD:** the forcing and boundary functional are more promising than abstract Gram positivity. An independently proved sign, modulus or localization restriction on that same arithmetic functional could add information. Merely replacing ζ(ρ)=0 by the equivalent plateau/boundary vanishing condition adds no independent restriction.

**Falsification results preserved / sharpened by literature:**

1. Multiplicity: k<m is essential; the first missing derivative reintroduces the boundary term. Do not use all jets at a simple zero.
2. Reflection: ρ and 1−ρ give the same κ node. Including both as distinct columns makes a determinant/independence claim false.
3. Generic nonreal forced resolvents already exist. Positive Stieltjes/Pick/Gram structure does not turn the forced Euler equation into an eigenvalue equation.
4. Signed self-dual gapped measures exist in the published literature. Off-lattice nonnegativity and the actual order-zero domain remain essential.
5. Squaring repairs sign but introduces the established nonzero duality defect; allowed lattice measures do not repair this displayed family.
6. Normalized near-gap comb limits can leave the measure category. A δ′/δ″ distribution cannot be fed into a Jordan-measure separator.
7. Fixed-order estimates do not imply MS1 along unbounded d(T); constants must be uniform at the needed rate.
8. A quiet-observation implication does not construct its quietness antecedent.
