## Primary literature that limits broad priority claims

Burnol's 2004 *Two complete and minimal systems associated with the zeros of the Riemann zeta function*, Theorems 3.1–3.3, explicitly treats evaluators indexed by 0≤k<mρ and dual systems involving ζ(s)/(s−ρ)^l for all available multiplicities. Thus “use all multiplicity jets associated to zeta zeros” is not new. An exact equivalence to the OPICA κ-normalized order-zero signed carriers has not been proved in this audit. [Published article](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.5802/jtnb.434/), [author's full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0203120).

Burnol's 2007 *Entrelacement de co-Poisson*, notably Theorem 4.9 and its gap/Mellin applications, gives distributional multiplicative-convolution/Fourier intertwining. It establishes substantial prior framework for co-Poisson and Fourier-gap constructions. This audit does not assert that it contains the precise OPICA off-integer positivity plus canonical-Jordan growth-domain separator. [Published article](https://www.numdam.org/item/AIF_2007__57_2_525_0/), [published full text](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/aif.2268.pdf).

Gautschi's 1962 *On inverses of Vandermonde and confluent Vandermonde matrices* defines derivative-column confluence and studies inverse bounds. The H2a algebra belongs to this established interpolation setting. The normalized 1/k! convention, κ row factor, affine substitution and grouped ordering determine the exact OPICA formula, not a new general mechanism. His inverse-bound perspective also warns that nonzero determinants alone do not give uniform conditioning near collisions. [Published article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01386302), [author-hosted full text](https://www.cs.purdue.edu/homes/wxg/selected_works/section_01/016.pdf).

Korevaar's authored *Tauberian Theory* supplies background for transform-boundary and growth arguments; it is not cited as proof that the exact OPICA C log T window or moving-order condition already appears there. No exact-priority theorem for those source-specific statements was verified here. [Monograph](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-662-10225-1).

The parent all-aperture derivative transport and signed sharp-gap separator remain the strongest earlier originality candidates identified by the initial screen. They predate these ten closures and must not be counted again as ten fresh mechanisms.
