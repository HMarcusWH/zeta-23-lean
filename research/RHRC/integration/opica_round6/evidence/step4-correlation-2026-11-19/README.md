## The exact target

Fix \(Y=10^7\). Group the Möbius divisors in \(\Lambda=\mu*\log\) by their squarefree factor supported on primes greater than \(Y\). Write \(c_r(t)\) for the signed contribution of group \(r\) to the genuine prime-power field \(P(t)\), so \(P=\sum_r c_r\), and put \(E_Y=\sum_r c_r^2\). The signed covariance \(P^2-E_Y\) retains every ordered pair of different groups. With \(w(t)=1/\log(2+t)\), the following would close Step 4:
\[
 \text{For arbitrarily large real }t,\qquad
 \int_{t-w(t)}^t\!(P(v)^2-E_Y(v))\,dv\le0.
\]
Arbitrarily large integer endpoints also suffice. An independently obtained sequence is required on either version. The merged native theorem supplies detection on logarithmically growing windows. The new paper argument uses Ingham's zero-density theorem and short filters to prove detection on these shrinking windows. Under a rightward zero the energy on **every** such late window has exponential growth, even when no zero attains the supremal displacement.
