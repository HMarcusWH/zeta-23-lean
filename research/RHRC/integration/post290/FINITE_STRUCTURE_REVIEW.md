# Finite carrier and observation review

The proposed M07 kernel uses the existing `poleEvenProfile`,
`poleOddProfile`, legal parity carriers and centered-index linear map.
Its compiler disposition is recorded in the component workflow harvest;
this document does not grant authority to an uncompiled source file.

The native pole profiles are nonzero scalar multiples of the paper's h and Dh.
The even profile is L/(16 pi^2) times h; the odd profile is 1/(4 pi) times Dh.
Their exact native index-functional identity is

    oddPole(Du) = -(L/(4 pi)) evenPole(u)

on the legal even carrier. The zero-sum condition removes the constant term.
Together with the existing legal D bijection, this gives a restricted
pole-kernel bijection for K >= 1. That statement also handles zero-dimensional
carriers algebraically; it constructs no eigenvalue for an empty carrier.
It does not make D unitary or compare spectra.

The other concrete kernel is a vector identity for the unchanged native matrix:
the canonical pole matrix annihilates simultaneous even/odd profile kernels.
On those vectors, canonicalSourceMatrix acts as minus its Archimedean action
minus its prime action. This gives no sign for the remaining signed channels.

M07 remains incomplete relative to the full specification: physical moment
equivalence, normal nonvanishing and exact K-2 dimensions still need their
full formal producer chain. M08-M14's corrected native compression, source
energy/residual bounds, strict odd adjoint, singular tie package, threshold

## Consequences and quickest next checks

DERIVED: the native profile normalization can be used instead of paper h
without changing the pole kernel. The product b alpha is invariant under a
common nonzero real rescaling of h and its even projected normal. This is a
useful normalization check before formalizing M08.

LEAD: the annihilated pole channel makes signed Archimedean/prime interaction
on the same source a cleaner independent estimate target. Absolute-value
bounds lose those cancellations. The full Gram matrix records the exact term
that a proposed cancellation claim must control.

Falsification is cheap: test the proposed estimate first on the unchanged
native seed and its D image, then on the source-matched modified-prime control.
A law surviving only positivity or generic rank-one geometry has not consumed
native arithmetic compatibility. A low-K positive sample supplies no evidence
for the theorem's unbounded quartic source schedule.

The original detector's deepest obstruction and a matching independent native
upper bound cannot yet be compared on a formally generated object. The
detector transport, corrected compressed source and complete retained Schur
producer are still missing. RH remains OPEN.
