# Frozen research and numerical protocol — proposed v1.0

**Status:** protocol specification, not an execution receipt. All numerical budgets below are proposed operational limits, not evidence that those limits will certify the domain.

## 1. Separate campaigns

Run three separate profiles: exact algebra/source regression; unconditional native finite observations; and D2 continuous-window continuation. None requires a known off-line zero. The formal conditional producer is audited through Lean, not instantiated by fabricating a zero.

The input source/toolchain/parameter protocol is frozen before confirmatory execution. An adaptive discovery pass may nominate later work but cannot change the frozen success predicates or discard failed points. New precision/method/domain proposals get a new protocol ID and an explicit comparison with the prior one.

## 2. Exact source and algebra regression

Replay the three exact post-#290 suites, preserving the original theorem and non-theorem scope. Add controls for the proposed strict-odd adjoint formulas and for the distinction between the one-step ground Schur complement and the multi-dimensional detector complement.

Minimum domains:

- Pole-neutral rational geometry: K in {3,4,5,6,7,8}; a in {1/16,1/4,1,4}. These are abstract rational geometry fixtures; a is not falsely labelled as the rational value of a native logarithmic aperture.
- Strict-even, strict-odd and tied rank-one linear-map fixtures with non-unitary D and exact rational entries. Include dimension 1, dimension 2 and a degenerate tied eigenspace of dimension at least 2.
- Native first-prime K3 control: exact L=(4/3)log2 source coordinate; rational algebra in a and pi with the proved interval inequalities. Preserve M4=-720 and the source/model mutation identifier.
- Threshold selector: repeated bottoms, no internal thresholds, thresholds on the selector interval endpoints, many duplicated values, first nonempty carrier and tied selected minima. Exact rational fixtures must verify both predecessor gaps.
- A two-dimensional detector trial whose complete complement has dimension greater than one; reject code that substitutes the selected one-step shell scalar for that detector complement.

An exact abstract control is not a native-zeta sample. Native arithmetic compatibility is checked only where the model actually supplies the integer and prime coefficients.

## 3. Native finite observation panel

Use the existing canonical matrix and source engines. Freeze:

- K in {3,4,6,8}.
- L in {1/512, 1/400, 1/4, 1/2, log2-1/1024, log2, log2+1/1024, log3, log4, log8, log16}.
- Both legal parity carriers and both minimal pole-neutral parity carriers.

There are 44 aperture/cutoff pairs and 176 carrier rows. Exact logarithmic expressions are preserved; interval evaluation does not replace them with decimal apertures. At seams, use the exact active-prime convention and prove the entering atom contribution rather than deciding the cutoff from an ambiguous floating floor.

For each pair produce:

1. Both bottom enclosures, within-parity cluster/gap information and the certified parity comparison, or UNRESOLVED.
2. Pole normals, projection maps, the corrected and uncorrected source, and their difference on the same vector.
3. Exact-construction trial vectors, their moments, Rayleigh value, residual, full native channel Gram matrix and scalar consistency checks.
4. Source/residual inequalities only where the hypotheses are certified; no arbitrary inverse in a tie or unresolved spectral ordering.
5. The native coefficient-level divisor residual through n=64, and the matched altered-prime control's first failed coefficient.

Vector policy:

- The pole-neutral K3 symbolic seed, zero-extended and normalized, is a supported probe for higher K.
- A higher-moment-null probe must be constructed through an exact constrained basis; if the required space is zero-dimensional, return NOT_APPLICABLE.
- A numerical ground vector is used only with an appropriate certified eigenspace enclosure. When a cluster is unresolved, use a projector/subspace output or mark vector-specific quantities UNRESOLVED.
- No native sampled vector is labelled as the hypothetical zero-generated detector merely because it is in the same carrier.

Precision policy: discovery at 192 bits, escalation to 384 and then 768 bits in that order. Confirmatory enclosures are independently checked at 1,024 bits when a delicate sign or spectral ordering is claimed. Cap discovery at 120 seconds per aperture/cutoff pair; independent point checking at 180 seconds per pair. On exhaustion emit a complete unresolved record, including last enclosure and reason. Do not manufacture lower bounds by dropping nonfinite values.

The theorem's K(L)=ceil(L^4) source schedule is not numerically certified by this low-K panel. A separate source evaluation may report the actual universal-sinc detector only if its infinite-product/convolution and quadrature tails are bounded. Otherwise emit the symbolic theorem-bound definition, not an unverified substitute bump. Alternate source scales used for development need distinct source IDs and cannot count as the fixed-source theorem's execution.

## 4. Research quantities and their interpretation

Calibration identities include the corrected intertwining defect, moment pairing, residual variance, complete-square Schur identity, and native divisor equation. Their theorem-consistent values are not new RH clues by themselves.

The useful comparisons are:

- Size and phase of the omitted pole-normal correction relative to the original S.
- The complete signed residual Gram sum versus its diagonal-only or triangle-bound surrogate.
- Original trial phase-plane quotient versus the ground quotient, with the exact loss in replacing one with the other.
- Complete Schur loss versus its diagonal and response components, each at its correct shift and normalization.
- Which proposed inequalities survive generic controls but fail after the genuinely native source compatibility is imposed, or vice versa.
- Finite adjoint-iteration residual decrease versus the unchanged projection onto the adjoint kernel.

A proposed candidate upper bound is entered with an explicit formula, constants' dependencies, parameter domain and matched-hypothesis list BEFORE the confirmatory panel is read. The runner returns HOLDS_ON_CHECKED_DOMAIN, FALSIFIED_ON_MATCHED_DOMAIN, or UNRESOLVED. None means a universal theorem. If a candidate matches an existing known identity after elimination, classify it as REFORMULATION_ONLY rather than a new discriminator.

## 5. D2 continuation: same SECOND method, explicit resource partition

### 5.1 Intake

Import the guarded source/run receipt from #290. Verify 30 layer IDs, 26 complete layers and four partial even layers. Verify the unresolved-list counts Q9=7, Q13=7, Q14=11, Q15=10 against the actual source-bound interval files. Do not reconstruct intervals from these counts or prose.

Preserve all original bytes and hashes. The actual endpoints and every accepted leaf are inputs to this campaign. If the archived artifacts cannot be recovered, report INPUT_MISSING; no replacement transcript may be invented.

### 5.2 Partition

Within each partial layer, sort unresolved closed rational intervals. Coalesce only exactly adjacent intervals; preserve their original interval IDs in the component ancestry. Reject overlaps/gaps that conflict with the inherited coverage semantics. Never extend a component into an unverified region.

Split each resulting component into eight equal rational pieces. The exact number of jobs is derived from this input; do not assume there are exactly four components merely because four layers are partial. Freeze the partition manifest before evaluation. Every child endpoint is an exact rational arithmetic expression from its parent.

### 5.3 First-pass budget

- Method: corrected whole-interval SECOND only.
- Precision: 256 bits.
- Maximum dyadic depth: 40 below each supplied subjob interval.
- Maximum visited nodes: 4,096 per subjob.
- Wall budget: 1,800 seconds per subjob.
- Maximum concurrent search jobs: 8.

Return accepted leaves and unresolved leaves with their precise reason. Coverage and the scientific result are distinct. A successful process may return an unresolved scientific disposition, but cannot satisfy D2's complete gate.

### 5.4 Predetermined second pass

For each first-pass unresolved leaf, partition once into four equal rational children. Run the same SECOND algorithm at 512 bits, depth 48, 8,192 nodes and 1,800 seconds per child, concurrency at most 8. This second-pass rule is part of the frozen protocol, not selected after inspecting preferred signs.

The total second-pass child cap is 256. If more are required, select the first 256 by (Q, original component index, rational left endpoint) for this bounded run, report all remaining leaves as unrun/UNRESOLVED, and keep D2 incomplete. No silent dropping or implicit additional resource authorization.

Do not invoke the historical FOURTH fallback. A new mathematical representation or derivative order requires a separately reviewed protocol, a new method ID and an explicit preservation/soundness proof.

### 5.5 Independent final checking

At 768 bits, independently reevaluate every final accepted leaf for all 30 layers, using current mathematical source definitions and the corrected full-interval curvature method. Prefer parallel no-search checking of archived leaves rather than rescouting the 26 completed layers. A replay check has its own current receipt; old receipts retain their original source identities.

Run the final global assembler and verifier in NON-PARTIAL mode. It must verify all interval unions, both parities, no missing layer, exact endpoint conventions and the full `[1/512, log16]` domain. The right rational enclosure must lie on the required side of log16 and be evaluated in a source/cutoff regime that is actually valid there; crossing a seam is not ignored.

D2_COMPLETE requires zero unresolved leaves, zero unrun leaves, zero missing artifacts, zero unverified intervals and no nonfinite enclosure. A certified negative native value is not an accepted positive leaf; it triggers an independently checked mathematical investigation.

### 5.6 Provenance rule

A current check can produce a new receipt consuming old raw intervals. It cannot rewrite an old receipt's commit/hash. If source equivalence is used instead of reevaluation, it must identify every changed mathematical input and certify equality in the relevant definitions; a display-only assertion is not a generic exemption.

14. Moving-pole-carrier derivative copied from fixed-carrier F04 without correction.

Where a mutation is mathematical rather than a data-format error, the test must exercise a Lean counterexample/failed type match or a matched exact mathematical fixture, not merely reject a string label.

## 7. Workflow accounting and post-green output

The final harvester consumes all pages and attempts of the workflow/job inventory for the exact tested head. It distinguishes latest-eligible final receipts from superseded attempts while preserving older failures. Unknown, queued, running, timed-out, cancelled and skipped jobs remain explicit.

The report must compare with the frozen post-#290 dispositions and the new protocol's preceding run. Explain whether a theorem changes the implication of an unchanged falsifier. Do not turn a green replay of an old negative result into proof of the candidate law it rejected.

Finish with a worked A-X, B-Y and C-Schur comparison using the newly proved outputs, and report the exact remaining inequality for each. Finite research completion is not a proof that the hypothetical packet cannot exist.
