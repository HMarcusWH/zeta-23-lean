## 3. What “output” means: three separate products

### 3.1 Formal conditional output

A Lean producer constructs a mathematical object under the actual off-line-zero hypothesis. It may use classical choice. It is **not** an algorithm that discovers or prints an off-line zeta zero.

Its proof fields must be derived from the source construction and existing theorems. The producer may not accept source visibility, finite transport, negative depth, source correction, or desired exclusion as extra unexplained premises.

### 3.2 Unconditional native evaluation output

An executable evaluates the actual canonical definitions at explicitly supplied valid finite parameters and vectors. Native positive samples are perfectly legitimate outputs. It must never require a negative native ground to exist merely to produce a report.

Exact source constraints and rigorous numerical resolution are separate fields. A floating approximation to a pole-neutral vector is not exactly pole-neutral without an algebraic construction or a certified residual with an explicit perturbation theorem.

### 3.3 Research handoff output

The final human-readable report and machine-readable records expose formal facts, inherited PAPER facts, finite evidence, failed proposed laws, numerical unresolved quantities, and genuinely OPEN closing bounds. They identify the exact source and scope for each.

A conditional theorem, a finite interval certificate, a numerical approximation and an inherited paper proof must never share a single undifferentiated `passed: true` flag.
