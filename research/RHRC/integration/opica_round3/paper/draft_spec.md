## 9. Falsification and anti-circularity checklist

- Can even and odd negative neutral sectors coexist for a genuine full matrix on some aperture? If yes, any proposed *universal* no-both theorem is false; an eventual variant needs a different test.
- Does the source-only index sign propagate to full Weil without exact correction? No: require the full prime/Archimedean identity and loss terms.
- Does cofinality of each parity alone imply common apertures? No: the union *cover* and openness are indispensable; formalize both.
- Does old global firstbad have PSD predecessor at neutral firstbad? Not necessarily; don't substitute it.
- Does a fixed set of Schwartz positive tests force positive arithmetic samples? No; prove the physical observation and its error.
- Does nonrange prove nondensity? No; both cases survive.
- Does finite Gram PD, small eigenvalue or finite certified residual prove the asymptotic best-error limit? No.
- Does a local Möbius identity imply a global L² approximation? No; missing tail bound.
- Does a full-causal range proof apply to the discrete native dictionary? No, the inclusion is strict.
- Does a bounded inverse control the full original-detector Schur if forcing grows? No.
- Does a K3 18-box replay cover every K/L? No.
- Does `P ⇒ RH` imply P? No. RH-equivalent rephrasings are not independent new mathematical constraints.
- Do generic Hermitian or positive-profile analogues that fail RH also satisfy the proposed constraint? If yes, evaluate whether the constraint is too generic to close.
