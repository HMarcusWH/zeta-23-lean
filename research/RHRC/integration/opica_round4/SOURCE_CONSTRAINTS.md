# Source constraints and the remaining arithmetic question

This extension is part of PR #298. It incorporates the mathematical work
originally scoped for a separate PR #299 into the existing Round 4 support gate.
The source/property comparison, exact contracts, state bindings and five open
arithmetic targets are in [SOURCE_CONSTRAINTS.json](SOURCE_CONSTRAINTS.json).
The [support register](ROOTS.json) lists the exact declarations; the
[math gate](math_gate.py) compiles their modules and independently checks their
complete types and transitive axioms. Source registration is not proof evidence.
Fresh production qualification remains necessary for the actual pushed head.

## What the extension adds

The genuine-source properties and the off-line-zero consequences concern the
**same canonical construction**. The comparison identifies freedoms that an
abstract counterexample might have but the actual construction does not. It
does not establish that the surviving genuine-source constraints contradict
one another.

1. [GroundArithmetic](../../../../Zeta23/OPICARound4/GroundArithmetic.lean)
   keeps the complete complex prime discrepancy. At a strictly lower even
   ground its value is the Archimedean/continuum row minus one. At a strictly
   lower odd ground the reciprocal even arithmetic input gives that row plus
   one. The odd eigenray and the reciprocal even input are distinct objects.
   Necessary source-budget and moment bounds retain their actual signs.
2. [JointGroundConstraints](../../../../Zeta23/OPICARound4/JointGroundConstraints.lean)
   joins the strict-even, strict-odd and tied alternatives at one aperture and
   cutoff. It carries absolute energy on actual eigenmodes. The equality case
   retains both kernels, range constraints and complete complementary responses
   with arbitrary multiplicity. An off-line zero produces these alternatives
   cofinally; this does not identify their cutoff with the separate quartic
   detector cutoff.
3. [SmoothForcing](../../../../Zeta23/OPICARound4/SmoothForcing.lean)
   represents the original smooth trial on the *same* actual predecessor as
   the stored finite forcing. Its pairing includes the full Archimedean term,
   complete signed prime contribution and the actual scalar shift.
4. [SchurErrorTransport](../../../../Zeta23/OPICARound4/SchurErrorTransport.lean)
   compares the full finite loss with that smooth expression. The bound keeps
   the inverse response error, the direct quadratic error and the shifted mass
   error. At cutoff three the predecessor forcing vanishes, while the direct
   energy and mass terms remain. The complement is not replaced by one shell.
5. [QuarticErrorReduction](../../../../Zeta23/OPICARound4/QuarticErrorReduction.lean)
   uses the actual quartic cutoff to make the complete error smaller than any
   fixed exponential scale. Thresholds are chosen before the late generated
   packet. The remaining smooth expression must still carry the large loss
   forced by the hypothetical zero.

Independent expected-type consumers are in
[GroundConstraints](../../../../Zeta23/OPICARound4/Validation/GroundConstraints.lean),
[DetectorErrors](../../../../Zeta23/OPICARound4/Validation/DetectorErrors.lean) and
[ErrorReduction](../../../../Zeta23/OPICARound4/Validation/ErrorReduction.lean).
The native gate also requires intentional semantic mistakes to fail by actual
type errors. A process, resource or import failure cannot satisfy that gate.

## The arithmetic still missing

The finite-to-smooth comparison removes approximation error as an explanation
of the surviving loss. It does not determine the sign of the complete smooth
source expression. The next mathematical task is an independent estimate of
that expression on the actual generated family, with the correct constants,
quantifiers and state bindings.

The shorter direct option remains available: the original smooth trial already
has an exponentially negative energy consequence under the zero hypothesis.
A source-specific lower bound incompatible with that energy on a sufficiently
late permitted packet would suffice. This route need not first solve every
ground or regular-cell obligation.

For the alternative ground route, a successful restriction must exclude all
three branches. Strict-gap estimates do not cover a shared negative ground.
The exact discrepancy identities also occur at positive strict grounds, so
they are necessary conditions, not separators by themselves. Source uniqueness,
finite probes, positivity in a different space and conditioning bounds alone
do not supply the missing arithmetic inequality.

**RH and Step 4 remain OPEN.** See the existing
[closure alternatives](CLOSURE_OBLIGATIONS.json); this extension neither assumes
nor registers an independently proved closing premise.
