# OPICA Round 4

Round 4 combines a governed deduction runtime with source-faithful mathematical
support and independent finite arithmetic probes. Read `MATH_CONTRACTS.json`
for the exact proved/partial/open boundary and `CLOSURE_OBLIGATIONS.json` for
the remaining alternatives. `ROOTS.json` registers 98 supporting declarations;
the native gate checks their full types, consumers and permitted axioms.

New support covers witness normalization and costs, the pole-neutral source
row, the absolute energy anchor, physical even/odd trace metrics, one-shell
negative index, signed response derivatives and one-sided norm bounds. The
original source definitions, theorem registries and compiler pins are preserved.
Existing aperture holomorphy is reused rather than counted as a new result.

The finite arithmetic lane checks six prescribed K4/K5 cases, including mixed
signed residues, the log(4) prime-power seam, an explicitly non-zeta modified
q=2 source, and entering q=2/q=4 atoms. Producers use 256-bit Arb balls;
independent 512-bit calculations use a different integration representation.
Intervals containing zero or overlapping unresolved spectra fail closed.
These cases do not establish an all-aperture or all-cutoff arithmetic bound.

The regular-cell chain remains incomplete: nonidentity and zero avoidance,
large-aperture separation, simultaneous interval selection, singular and
shared-ground alternatives still need source-specific theorems. The final
Stieltjes change of variables, grouped native spectral expansion and joint
moving-test arithmetic error budget remain open. The direct eventual
no-simultaneous-negativity route, causal approximation, complete signed
observation and direct detector complement remain separate open alternatives.

`BUILD_MANIFEST.json` closes the source delta at the merged PR #297 tree.

current compatibility checks the complete 462-declaration/696-membership
inventory on the actual child. The new eleven-lane aggregate is separate
from the old acceptance workflow. Source-only census entries and unreviewed
contacts retain their previous dispositions.
