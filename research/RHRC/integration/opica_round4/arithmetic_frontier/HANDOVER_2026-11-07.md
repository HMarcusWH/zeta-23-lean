## Experimental signals and leads (not theorems)

* **Small near-band coupling is not a sign criterion.**
  * A low-block perturbation leaves `C` and `A` identical and makes the
    pencil negative.
  * A low-band off-line world is certified negative with a coupling bound
    within 5% of the native one.
* **Off-line detection threshold.**
  * `ε*(δ) ≈ c(L)·δ²`, with `c ≈ 9.4, 17.9, 28.2` (low band) at L=4, 5, 6.
  * The `1/2` and `1/3` checkpoints are blind to `δ = 1/10` quartets; the
    L=6 `1/4` checkpoint detects them, as predicted in session.
  * Fixed-margin detection is already proved in Lean.
* **Explicit-formula calibration.** The native form equals the truncated
  zero sum `Σ_ρ Re conj(U(−w̄))U(w)` to `1.7e-5` (odd) and `1.1e-2` (even)
  with 400 zeros at L=2.
* **Signed source.**
  * On the near-null subspace, `−arch` and `−prime` are each indefinite with
    O(1) eigenvalues and cancel to at most `7.5e-3`.
  * The whole prime cell is needed: dropping only the largest prime power
    destroys positivity.
  * No decomposition into independently controlled terms was found. This
    reads as a sharp interface with Weil positivity, not a sub-RH step.
* **Near-null count.** `dim − N_main(2πK/L)` within a few units (CR22).
