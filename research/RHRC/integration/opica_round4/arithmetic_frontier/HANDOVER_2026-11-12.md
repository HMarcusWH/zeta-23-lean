## 1. Where things stand

The 11 November handover asked for a way to close Step 4. Round 7 did not
close it. Its main finding is a reduction.

* The three alternative suppliers listed in November, (U), (F) and (E), are
  one growth bound for the complete prime field `Z`, measured in three norms.
  At a rate `α` that bound excludes zeros of displacement above `α` and is
  implied by their absence (DERIVED).
* The same statement for the native slice floors is compiled in this increment
  (the rate ladder). The slice contracts (C1), (SB), (PG) and (SE) are each
  equivalent to RH (PROVED, local gate).

The remaining premise is therefore classical, and it can be stated on one
explicit sequence of smoothed prime sums. Partial progress would be a rate
below `1/2`, which is a zero-free strip of fixed width (the quasi-Riemann
hypothesis). That too is open in the established literature.

## 2. What became formally true (local pinned gate; current-head CI pending)

One module, `Zeta23/OPICARound4/ArithmeticFrontier/ZeroFreeFloor.lean`,
carries contracts M60 and M61 (46 declarations). The local gate passed at 108
modules, 1367 complete types and axiom sets and 119 rejection controls (84 +

and 12 use `propext, Quot.sound`. No other axiom occurs.

M60 has three parts.

1. **Floor with constants.** If every nontrivial zero has `|Re ρ − 1/2| ≤ a`,
   then on every boundary-flat native vector at aperture `L` and cutoff `K`:

       Re⟪Q x, x⟫ ≥ −S e^{aL} · L (1 + (2πK/L)²)² (2K + 1) · ‖x‖²,   S = ∑ m_ρ / (1 + |γ_ρ|²)

   (`canonicalSource_re_inner_ge_neg_of_zeroStrip`). Both moment slices
   inherit it. At the quartic cutoff the budget is at most `128000 (1 + L)^17`
   (`nativeFloorBudget_quartic_le`).
2. **Rate ladder.** For `a ≥ 0`, in each parity, the strip of half-width `a`
   holds if and only if every rate above `a` is a fixed-rate slice bound
   (`zeroStrip_iff_even_fixed_rates`, `zeroStrip_iff_odd_fixed_rates`).
   * A half-plane `Re ρ ≤ θ` gives every rate above `θ − 1/2`
     (`fixedRateMomentSliceBounds_of_re_le`).
   * The closed strip alone gives every rate above `1/2`
     (`fixedRateMomentSliceBounds_of_half_lt`). No better rate is known
     unconditionally.
3. **Sharp interfaces.** Under RH the repository's critical-line positivity
   gives both slice bounds with error zero, at every aperture and cutoff
   (`momentSliceLowerBounds_zero_of_riemannHypothesis`). With the compiled
   consumers this gives four equivalences with Mathlib `RiemannHypothesis`:
   * `riemannHypothesis_iff_cofinal_moment_slice_bounds` (C1);
   * `riemannHypothesis_iff_cofinal_moment_slice_semibounded` (SB);
   * `riemannHypothesis_iff_fixed_parity_polynomial` (PG);
   * `riemannHypothesis_iff_fixed_parity_subexponential` (SE).

M61, the last section of the same module, is a finite selector statement. If
the cutoff-3 global bottom is below `−D/2`, every
`NativeDeepThresholdGroundData` packet has cutoff 3 and both predecessor spaces
have dimension zero
(`nativeDeepThresholdGround_cutoff_eq_three_of_bottom_three_lt`). Four
companions give the positive depth, the empty predecessor, and the even and odd
one-vector forms.

The six new controls reject:

* the floor at half its rate;
* the rate `a` itself instead of every rate above it;
* a half-plane read with `θ − 1`;
* every positive rate presented as unconditional;
* the cutoff-3 threshold `−D/3`;
* (PG) presented without its RH premise.

These are adapters and one finite statement. They bound no displacement.

## 3. Paper results of round 7 (DERIVED unless marked)

Source: [round7/ROUND7_SELECTED_PREDECESSOR_AND_SCALAR_TARGET.md](round7/ROUND7_SELECTED_PREDECESSOR_AND_SCALAR_TARGET.md)
(repository edition of the Drive note) and
[round7/TARGET_CONSOLIDATION.md](round7/TARGET_CONSOLIDATION.md).

* **Explicit formula in closed form.** On boundary-flat vectors the energy is a
  sum over zeros of `sinh²` times the squared vector transform (the identity
  itself is compiled: `zeta_W_boundaryFlatFiniteVector_eq_canonicalSourceQuadraticForm`).
  The two cutoff-3 vectors have explicit transforms.
* **Selector under a hypothetical zero** of displacement `δ`.
  * For every `β < δ` there are arbitrarily late apertures where a cutoff-3 unit
    vector has energy below `−e^{βL}`. There the predecessor is `{0}`.
  * With a dominant zero the cutoff is 3 or 4 at every late aperture.
  * Caveat: without dominance, the note does not show that these apertures are
    the ones the compiled production detector selects.
* **Target consolidation.** (U), (F) and (E) are the weighted `L¹`, weighted
  `L²` and plain `L²` growth bounds of `Z`. At rate `η` each excludes zeros of
  displacement above `η` and is implied by their absence.
* **Scalar criterion.** `sup Re ρ − 1/2` is the least exponential rate at which
  the even cutoff-3 energy `E_e(L)` is bounded below. Under RH,
  `0 < E_e(L) ≤ 12.94 / L⁵`.
* **Explicit floor with a verification height.** This uses the published height
  `H₀ = 3·10¹²` (an external computation). With the trivial `θ₀ ≤ 1` the floor
  on the whole quartic carrier stays above `−1` up to about `L = 86`, then
  degrades like `e^{L/2}`.
* **Real-root lemma.** A simple bottom has a ground transform whose zeros `z`
  have `z²` real. It also holds for forms with hugely negative ground energy,
  so it cannot separate RH from its failure.
* **Ground-state convergence (LEAD).** Identifying the near-radical vector with
  the ground vector needs the absence of lower eigenvalues, which is canonical
  positivity. The lead consumes the missing estimate.
